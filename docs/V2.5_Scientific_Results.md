> **Methodological Note on the 5.25 Threshold:**
> The Set-Level Sufficiency threshold of 5.25 is accurately described as the "Dev-set threshold selected under the constraint of zero observed OOD leakage." With only 5 OOD examples in the Dev set, achieving 100% OOD recall is strong empirical performance but does not statistically guarantee that the true leakage probability is zero. It should not be interpreted as a universally "optimal" threshold.
